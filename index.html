Musical Circles

A world of a hundred spheres, each one a note. Where a sphere sits decides how it sounds: higher is brighter, nearer is louder, bigger is deeper.

Headphones recommended

How to play

  • Click a sphere to sound it
  • Drag it up or down to slide through the scale
  • ↑ ↓ resize the sphere under the cursor: bigger is lower and longer
  • Drag the background to orbit, scroll to zoom
  • H hides this panel
